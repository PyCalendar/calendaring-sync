Every public method of ``Replica``, ``Session`` and the stores, and every value an adapter or application builds, now checks its argument types and raises ``TypeError`` for a wrong one the same way on every store; a subclass of ``str`` or ``int``, such as an enum, counts as wrong. Before, a wrong type could be stored as it was by one store and converted by another, such as ``delete_record("1")``, which deleted record 1 only on SQLite.
