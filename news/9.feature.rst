Added pushes and conflict resolution: a :class:`~calendaring_sync.session.Session` claims each pending edit before it's sent, so no other process sends it at the same time, and records how the push ended. :meth:`Replica.resolve() <calendaring_sync.replica.Replica.resolve>` and :meth:`Replica.resolve_conflicts() <calendaring_sync.replica.Replica.resolve_conflicts>` resolve a conflict with your edit, the server's side, merged content, or a delete.
