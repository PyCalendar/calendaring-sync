Added pushes and conflict resolution: a :class:`~calendaring_sync.session.Session` claims each pending edit before it's sent, so no other process sends it at the same time, and records how the push ended. :meth:`Replica.resolve() <calendaring_sync.replica.Replica.resolve>` resolves one conflict with your edit, the server's copy, merged content, or a delete, and :meth:`Replica.resolve_conflicts() <calendaring_sync.replica.Replica.resolve_conflicts>` resolves every open one with your function and returns which it resolved, left open, or found changed.
