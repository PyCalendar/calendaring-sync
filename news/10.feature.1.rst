Added the CalDAV adapter: ``CalDAVAdapter`` and ``AsyncCalDAVAdapter`` sync one calendar collection through python-caldav 3.6.0 or later, installed with the ``caldav`` extra, and their ``create`` and ``change`` record your edits with the fingerprint and UID a sync would give them, while ``waiting()`` lists the edits they can't send yet. A calendar the server no longer has raises the new ``ScopeGoneError``, and a full listing the server cuts short raises ``IncompleteListingError``.
