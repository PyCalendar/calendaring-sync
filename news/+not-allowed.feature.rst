A push the server won't take as it was sent, such as a write to a calendar you may only read or content the calendar won't store, ends :class:`~calendaring_sync.objects.push.NotAllowed` and becomes a conflict of kind :attr:`~calendaring_sync.objects.conflict.ConflictKind.NOT_ALLOWED`, with the server's explanation in :attr:`Conflict.reason <calendaring_sync.objects.conflict.Conflict.reason>`, instead of stopping every sync; each adapter maps its protocol's refusals to it.
